# "Tell me about yourself": prep template

He'll very likely ask some version of this. Don't memorize word-for-word. Know the shape of the answer so it comes out naturally. Fill in each blank, say it out loud a couple of times, then let it go loose in the actual conversation.

## 1. Background (2-3 sentences)
Who you are academically right now, in plain terms.

> "I'm currently [year/degree] in [program], and I took your MTH3230 unit in [semester/year], that's actually where I first got interested in [stochastic processes / time series / whichever part clicked for you]. My strongest areas so far are [subjects, e.g. differential equations, probability, linear algebra], and [one honest gap, e.g. "I haven't done much biology yet, so that's new ground for me"]."

**Fill in:**
- Program/year: __________
- What clicked for you in MTH3230: __________
- Strongest subjects: __________
- Honest gap: __________

## 2. Why this area interests you (2-3 sentences)
Not "because it sounds impressive". Give a real reason, even a small one.

> "What draws me to [specific topic, e.g. stochastic modelling of gene networks] is [reason, could be a specific idea, a connection to something you've studied, curiosity about the randomness/noise angle, interest in applying math to real biological problems, etc.]."

**Fill in:**
- Specific topic that interests you most: __________
- Real reason why: __________

## 3. Why him / why this project (1-2 sentences)
Ties it back to the meeting itself.

> "I wanted to talk to you specifically because [your work on X connects directly to what I learned in MTH3230 / I'm drawn to the computational biology side / etc.], and I'd like to understand what a project with you would actually involve."

**Fill in:**
- Your specific reason: __________

## 4. A rough idea or question of your own (optional but strong)
Even a tentative, half-formed idea shows initiative rather than pure receptiveness.

> "One thing I've been wondering about is [a genuine question, e.g. 'how do you decide when a stochastic model is actually necessary versus a deterministic one being good enough?']."

**Fill in:**
- Your own question or angle: __________

---

**Note:** you don't need to say all four in order like a script. Just have the pieces ready so you're not caught flat-footed if he asks directly.
